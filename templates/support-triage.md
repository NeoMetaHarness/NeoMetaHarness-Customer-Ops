# Support triage — extension will not enable

## Intake
Record product version, platform, extension ID/version, observed error, expected behavior and reproduction steps. Request redacted logs only.

## Diagnosis
Check declared dependencies, extension health and permission failures. Verify the failure in an isolated profile before escalating.

## Resolution record
Capture diagnostic evidence, safe workaround, fix owner, affected versions, and verification result.

## Privacy
Do not paste API keys, personal content or raw vault data into public issues. Route security reports through the private disclosure process.
